MAPS sheets. Prototype, throwaway.

One layout for every entity, worked out from why the earlier drawings were a mess. Each sheet is computed from the same states-and-verbs data as before; nothing is placed by hand.

  • Home is the anchor. Each part of the sheet has one home state, the one the entity rests in, at the left edge.
  • Getting back home is never drawn. Everything inside a part can return home, so a move that ends at home is a small ↩ note under the state it leaves from. Most lines in the old drawings were these returns.
  • Everything else reads left to right as a sentence. Verb, the state it leads to, the next verb, and so on. The column is how many moves from home. A fork starts a new line under its parent, like an outline.
  • Parts stack top to bottom, split at points of no return. Down only ever means you can't come back.
