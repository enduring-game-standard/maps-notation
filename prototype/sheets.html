MAPS sheets, v2. Prototype, throwaway.

The same four rules as v1, with what falsifying v1 broke now fixed:

  • Home is the anchor, and the designer names it (⌂), the way a score names its key. v1 guessed homes and got all four Deku Tree parts wrong.
  • Getting back is a note, never a line, and it keeps its verb and who says it: ↩ home, ↰ one step back, ↻ stays. v1's ⟷ hid the way back and made the roll look player-ended.
  • Everything else reads left to right as a sentence. The column is moves from home. The longest branch runs straight on, and shorter ones fork below it, so listing the verbs in another order draws the same sheet.
  • Parts stack top to bottom, split at points of no return (⇓). Down only ever means you can't come back.
  • Under each verb, the circumstance that allows it, never a button.
