The #14 design, drawn twice. Prototype, throwaway.

The same data, two fundamentally different drawings. Switch with the bar at the bottom, or keys 1 and 2. A, the diagram: a net with a fixed grammar. Home is at the top, down is further from home, a heavy bar is no return, and both arcs of every verb are drawn. B, the score: fixed staves, one row per verb, read left to right as the conversation. The controller observes, says the verb, and the world applies it.
