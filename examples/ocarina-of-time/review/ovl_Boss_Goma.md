# ovl_Boss_Goma

Reviewer: Claude Sonnet 5.5 (claude-sonnet-5-5)

Spec: `studio/tools/ocarina/data/actors/boss_goma.ts`. Source: `src/overlays/actors/ovl_Boss_Goma/z_boss_goma.c` (`Z` below). Frame-length claims that depend on animation lengths (swing, recover) were not checked; the animation assets were not read.

## Verdict

major findings

## Findings

1. **Steps `stunned_nut -> floor_main_retreat` and `stunned_seed -> floor_main_retreat` (cite 1445).** Spec: when a Deku Nut or seed stun times out, Gohma goes to the retreat phase (out of patience). Code: `BossGoma_FloorStunned` ends with `BossGoma_SetupFloorMain` (Z:1444-1445). Whether she then retreats depends on `patienceTimer == 0`. `patienceTimer` is zeroed only at the end of `BossGoma_FloorDamaged` (Z:1390, after a sword hit) and in `FloorLandStruckDown` (Z:1409). The nut/seed stun path (Z:1868-1883) does not touch it. An un-hit nut or seed stun therefore returns to `FloorMain` with its remaining patience (the patient chase branch, Z:1642-1650). It does not retreat. Only a stun in which a sword hit landed retreats. The spec sends every nut or seed stun to retreat, so a Link who stuns her and does not hit loses nothing in the model and gains a free retreat. Severity: major (changes the loop Link faces and who can win).

2. **Attacks: contact damage in other phases (`attacks`, `atSources`).** Spec: the only damage to Link is `claw`, delivered by step `attack -> rest` (near). Code: all 13 elements are `ATELEM_ON` with damage 8 (Z:100-232) and `CollisionCheck_SetAT` runs every frame except in `FloorStunned`, `FloorDamaged`, and `FloorMain` while `timer != 0` (Z:1979-1982). So her body hurts Link in `floor_main_patient`, `posture`, `rest`, `floor_main_retreat` (timer 0), `wall_climb`, the ceiling phases, `fall` and `crash`, not just during the swing step. No phase other than `attack` can hurt Link in the model. Severity: major (what hurts).

3. **Step `attack -> rest`, phase `rest` frames [30,59] (cite 1354).** Spec: every swipe ends in a rest of 30-59 frames. Code: the rest timer is set only when `framesUntilNextAction == 0` at the end of the swing (Z:1353-1355). Each frame the swing hits Link it sets `framesUntilNextAction = 10` (Z:1337-1338), so a hit in the last 10 frames of the swing skips the rest and goes straight to recover (Z:1359-1364). The spec applies the 30-59 rest to the hit case with no distinction. Whether a hit usually lands that late depends on the swing animation length, which I did not read. Severity: minor (timing).

4. **Phase `ceiling_laying`, frames 64 (cite 1539).** Spec: laying lasts 64 frames. Code: 64 is the exit threshold of `spawnGohmasActionTimer` (Z:1539), but the timer is reset to 23 after each of the first two spawns (Z:1534-1536). The spawn fires when `tailLimbsScaleTimers[0] == 2`, set to 10 at timer 48 (Z:1520-1523, 1527-1531). By my count from those lines that is timer 56 for the first egg, 33 more frames for each of the next two, and 8 more to 64, roughly 130 frames. The count is my derivation, not a line. Severity: minor (timing).

5. **Phase `ceiling_idle_larvae`: `hittable: 'none'`, and step `-> fall` (cite 1592).** Spec (and `deadVerbs` note): the eye is shut while larvae live (Z:1768), so no hit moves her, and the timer is the only exit. Code: the eye closes only while some `childrenGohmaState > 0` (Z:1768-1770). Once every child is dead (all `< 0`) the eye reopens, and Gohma stays on the ceiling for the rest of `CeilingIdle`'s 20-49 frames (Z:475, 1585-1592). A hit then still hits `CeilingIdle` and drops her into `FallStruckDown` (Z:1850-1852). Also, with children alive she cycles `CeilingIdle -> CeilingMoveToCenter` (Z:1603-1604) and back (Z:1737-1739). The spec has no such step. When the last child dies in `CeilingMoveToCenter` she stays on the ceiling for the rest of its 30-89 frames (Z:536), plus another idle, before `FallJump`, and her eye is open (hit-eligible, as in `ceiling_move`) throughout. This window is not in the model. Severity: minor (timing; a narrow window).

6. **Missing step `floor_main_patient -> wall_climb` (Z:1674-1675).** Spec: `wall_climb` is entered only from `floor_main_retreat`. Code: `FloorMain` calls `SetupWallClimb` on any wall contact, with or without patience (Z:1674-1676). A patient Gohma that reaches a wall climbs to the ceiling without retreating. Severity: minor (a real path to the ceiling phases, but chance-dependent on Link's position, and the model reaches the same phases through retreat).

7. **Phases `stunned_nut` and `stunned_seed`, `hittable: SWORD_TYPES` only.** Spec: only sword-type hits connect during these stuns. Code: a Deku Nut or seed hit also re-triggers the stun whenever `actionFunc != FloorStunned` and `patienceTimer != 0` (Z:1868-1869). `FloorDamaged` is mapped to `stunned_*`, and after a nut/seed stun plus a sword hit `patienceTimer` is still nonzero until `FloorDamaged` ends (Z:1390). During that window (outside the 10 invincibility frames, Z:1867) a second nut or seed re-enters `FloorStunned` and reloads `framesUntilNextAction` to 40 or 90 (Z:1873-1882). Severity: minor (narrow window).

8. **Step `floor_main_patient -> posture` versus `posture -> attack`, both `range: 'near'` (cite 1646, 1300).** The first needs `xzDistToPlayer < 150` (Z:1645), the second `< 250` (Z:1299). One range band stands for two thresholds, so a Link in the 150-250 band is modeled as the same distance for both. This is a modeling choice and the spec does not say so. Severity: minor (labeling).

## Outcomes
1. fixed: split each nut/seed stun by patience. `stunned_nut` and `stunned_seed` (patience left) now time out to `floor_main_patient` (Z:1445, patience untouched by Z:1868-1883). A sword hit there moves to new phases `stunned_nut_spent` / `stunned_seed_spent` (override cite Z:1390, patience zeroed when FloorDamaged ends), which time out to `floor_main_retreat`. The spent copy restarts the stun timer because the types cannot carry framesUntilNextAction across phases (Z:611-615); recorded in the spec header.
2. fixed: the claw attack is now `contact` (same 13 elements, 8 damage, Z:100-232). Near steps deliver it out of every phase where SetAT runs and Link can be near (Z:1979-1981): patient->posture, posture->attack, attack->rest, a new rest self-step, retreat->wall_climb, wall_climb->ceiling_move, fall->floor_main_patient, crash->stunned_struck (each split near/far). Ceiling phases get none: she is on the ceiling there (Z:1698, y > -320), out of Link's reach. Stun phases get none (Z:1979-1980).
3. gap: a hit in the last 10 frames of the swing skips the rest (Z:1337-1338, 1353-1355). The whole swing resolves in one beat, so sub-beat hit timing is not expressible; recorded in the spec header.
4. fixed: `ceiling_laying` frames 64 -> 130 (7 beats). Derivation: timer counts from 0 (Z:507); tail timer set to 10 at 48 (Z:1520-1522) and decremented after the action each frame (Z:1830-1831, called at Z:1969), so the spawn check `== 2` (Z:1526) fires at 56; the first two spawns reset to 23 (Z:1534-1535), giving 33 frames each; after the third, 8 more to 64 (Z:1539). 56+33+33+8 = 130. Cite stays Z:1539; the derivation is in this note.
5. fixed: `ceiling_idle_larvae` now leads to a new phase `ceiling_idle_clear` (eye open, Deku Nut knocks her down via Z:1850-1852, frames [20,49] Z:475), then `fall` (Z:1592). The idle/move cycle while larvae live (Z:1603-1604, 1737-1739) is folded into `ceiling_idle_larvae` since the eye is shut in both (Z:1768). The clear window's real length is the remainder of the current idle or move; modeled as one idle. Recorded in the spec header.
6. gap: wall contact (Z:1674-1675) is room geometry. A patient Gohma walks toward Link, so the climb is not inevitable, and an unconditional step would let her climb any beat. Recorded in the spec header.
7. gap: FloorDamaged is folded into the stun phases, so the nut/seed re-stun window during it (Z:1868-1873, which also skips the Z:1390 patience reset) is not modeled. Recorded in the spec header.
8. gap: Step.range has one near band (spec.ts Step.range `'near' | 'far'`), so the 150 (Z:1645) and 250 (Z:1299) thresholds share it. Recorded in the spec header.

Also: sword-table cites moved to the value lines z_collision_check.c:3719/3721/3723/3725 (same issue as the En_Goma finding 2).
